How do I study for CS 50 at Harvard University?

Record each CS 50 lecture on-device with Cram Vault. It builds a 1-page cheat sheet with timestamps and FSRS flashcards that resurface what you are about to forget before the midterm.

Is Cram Vault affiliated with Harvard University?

No. Cram Vault is an independent study app; course codes are listed only so Harvard University students can find relevant starter decks.

Does it work offline in the lecture hall?

Yes. Transcription, the whiteboard scanner and flashcard scheduling all run on-device.
