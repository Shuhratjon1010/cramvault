How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Cram Vault › Cancel.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Cram Vault › Settings › Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete All Data in the app, or email https://github.com/Shuhratjon1010/cramvault/issues.

How do I use Siri?

Say “Hey Siri, start recording lecture in Cram Vault” or “Quiz me for my next exam in Cram Vault”.
