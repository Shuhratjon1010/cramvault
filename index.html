FAQ

What is Cram Vault?

Cram Vault is an iPhone and iPad app that records lectures, transcribes them on-device, and generates AI flashcards, practice quizzes, formula sheets and a one-page exam cheat sheet.

Is Cram Vault free?

Yes, Cram Vault is free to download and try. Cram Vault Pro unlocks unlimited lectures, AI flashcards, quizzes and cheat sheet export.

Does recording leave my phone?

No. Recording and transcription run on-device by default.

Which exams is Cram Vault good for?

College courses, AP, SAT, MCAT, NCLEX-RN, LSAT and the Bar Exam.
