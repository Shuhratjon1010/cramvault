Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026.

Data we process

Lecture audio, transcripts, flashcards and notes are created and stored on your device. Microphone, speech recognition and camera access are used only when you start a recording or scan. If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your private iCloud account, which we cannot access. Optional AI generation sends transcript text over an encrypted connection to generate study material; it is not used to train models and is not retained after processing.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment card details.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregated crash and usage diagnostics. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising tracking.

Children

Cram Vault is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights & deletion

Delete any lecture in the app, or delete all data in Settings › Delete All Data. Contact https://github.com/Shuhratjon1010/cramvault/issues for access or deletion requests (GDPR/CCPA).
